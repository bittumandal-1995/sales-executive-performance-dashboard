📊 Sales Executive Performance Dashboard
🚀 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales executive performance across multiple regions using an interactive Excel dashboard. The goal was to convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights that help management track performance, identify trends, and improve decision-making.

**❗ Business Problem**

**The company was facing difficulties in:**

Tracking regional sales performance
Identifying top and low-performing executives
Monitoring target achievement percentages
Comparing sales trends across cities
Making quick decisions using raw spreadsheets

The existing reporting process lacked proper visualization and actionable insights.

**✅ Solution Implemented**

To solve these problems, I created an interactive Excel dashboard featuring:

KPI Cards for Total Sales & Target Achievement
Regional Performance Analysis
Executive-wise Sales Comparison
Bar Charts, Pie Charts & Trend Analysis
Interactive Filters & Slicers
Dynamic Reporting Dashboard

The dashboard transformed raw sales records into a clean and easy-to-understand business reporting system.

**📈 Key Insights Generated**
Certain regions consistently outperformed others in sales.
A small group of executives contributed a major portion of revenue.
Some regions showed unstable target achievement trends.
Sales performance varied significantly across locations.
Performance gaps highlighted areas requiring management attention.
**💡 Business Recommendations**
Provide additional support and training to underperforming regions.
Reevaluate unrealistic sales targets across certain locations.
Replicate strategies used by top-performing executives.
Introduce performance-based incentives to improve productivity.
Use dashboard-driven monitoring for faster business decisions.
